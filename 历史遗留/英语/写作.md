# 英语错题集 · 写作

---

## 第 1 题

**日期：** 2026-10-18  
**知识点：** 一般过去时 + 字数达标 + 结尾不反问读者  
**题型：** 命题作文（An enjoyable sport moment，不少于50词）

### 题目

Write at least 50 words about the topic "An enjoyable sport moment".  
Suggested questions:
- What sport did you do?
- How did you do that sport?
- Why did you think it is enjoyable?

### 我的答案

> Hi! I am Hanson. I am 11 years old. Last Sunday, I **play** badminton with my father. My father is good at playing it, so I want to beat him very much. That day, I beat my father. I am very happy. **What is your enjoyable sport moment? Why?**

### 错误原因

1. **时态错误**：Last Sunday 是过去时间，"I play badminton" 应该用过去式 **played**。
2. **字数不够**：约42词，要求不少于50词，差8词左右。
3. **结尾不当**：最后两句 "What is your enjoyable sport moment? Why?" 是反问读者，作文里不应该这样写，应该收尾在自己的感受上。
4. **细节不够**：只说 "I am very happy"，没有写比赛过程、比分、为什么开心。

### 正确答案参考（约60词）

> Hi! I am Hanson. I am 11 years old. Last Sunday, I **played** badminton with my father. My father is good at playing it, so I wanted to beat him very much. We played for an hour. The score was 18-19, and I was behind. I took a deep breath and tried my best. Finally, I won 21-19! I was so happy and excited. It was really an enjoyable sport moment.

### 举一反三

- **写过去的事，全文用过去时**：played, wanted, was, won。看到 Last Sunday / Yesterday / Last year，动词立刻变身。
- **凑字数小技巧**：加细节——比分（18-19）、动作（took a deep breath）、感受（so happy and excited）。
- **作文结尾**：收尾在自己的感受或总结上，比如 "It was really an enjoyable sport moment." 不要反问读者。
- **三段式结构**：
  1. 开头：什么运动、和谁、什么时候
  2. 中间：比赛过程、发生了什么
  3. 结尾：感受、为什么 enjoyable
